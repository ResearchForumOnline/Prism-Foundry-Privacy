# Prism Foundry — Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 2 October 2026**  
**Product:** Prism Foundry, a Windows game by TalkToAI

This notice explains how the Prism Foundry game handles information.

## Information handled by the game

Prism Foundry stores gameplay progress and settings locally in the game's save area on your device. This may include board progress, scores, preferences, and other gameplay state needed to resume play. The game does not send this local save data to TalkToAI.

The game does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, online multiplayer, or in-game purchases.

You can remove local game data through Windows app-storage controls or by uninstalling the game, subject to Windows' handling of app data.

## Microsoft Store and Windows services

When you install or purchase Prism Foundry through Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the transaction, licensing, installation, and related platform services under Microsoft's own privacy statements and terms. Windows may process diagnostic or device data according to the settings and services you use. These platform practices are separate from the game's local-save behavior. See the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Accounts and children

Prism Foundry does not ask players to create an account or enter personal information to play.

## Contact

For questions about this notice or the game's privacy practices, contact [shaf@talktoai.org](mailto:shaf@talktoai.org?subject=Prism%20Foundry%20privacy).
